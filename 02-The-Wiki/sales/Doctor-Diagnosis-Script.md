# Doctor Diagnosis Sales Script
## Sabri Suby — Sell Like Crazy Framework
## Applied to VIP Premier Painting Color Consultations

## Core Philosophy
Treat every prospect like a patient.
DIAGNOSE before you PRESCRIBE.
Never recommend a solution before fully understanding the problem.

## 5 Stages

### STAGE 1: PATTERN INTERRUPT (Email/Text Reply)
Goal: Stop them treating this like a commodity.

"Hi {{first_name}},
Really glad the concepts resonated — we designed those specifically
around your home's {{style}} architecture.
Before I go further, I want to make sure I give you the right
information for your specific situation. Quick questions:
1. Exterior, interior, or both?
2. Timeline you're working toward?
3. Biggest concern — color choice, finding the right team, or process?
Once I understand where you are, I'll tell you exactly what we'd
recommend — and whether VIP Premier is the right fit."

### STAGE 2: DIAGNOSIS (Phone Script)
Opening: "Thanks for the time. Before I say anything about what we do —
can I ask a few questions to understand your situation better? I want
to make sure I give you the right info."

[Wait for yes]

"Tell me — what's going on with the house right now? What made you reach out?"
[LISTEN — do not interrupt]

Questions (pick 3-4):
□ "How long have you been thinking about this?"
□ "What's the main thing holding you back?"
□ "Have you gotten other quotes? What was that like?"
□ "What would the perfect result look like for you?"
□ "Who else is involved in this decision?"
□ "Is there an event or timeline you're working toward?"

Listen for:
• Urgency: HOA notice, selling soon, event coming
• Budget signal: "we want to do it right" = price insensitive
• Emotional driver: pride, fear of regret, neighbor comparison
• Decision maker: need spouse on call?

### STAGE 3: PRESCRIPTION
Only after full diagnosis.

"Based on what you've told me, here's what I'd recommend.
Your home is {{style}} — that architecture works beautifully with [Scheme X].
The {{feature}} will really come alive with [palette direction].
What I'd suggest: 30-minute consultation at your home. I'll bring
Sherwin-Williams physical samples that match your renders, and we'll
look at them in context on your property.
Most homeowners tell me within 10 minutes they know exactly which
direction they want. It just takes seeing it on site.
Does that sound useful?"

### STAGE 4: OBJECTION HANDLING

"Need to think about it"
→ "Of course. What specifically — timing, investment, or something else?"
→ Timing: "What's your timeline? This season or further out?"
→ Investment: "We also offer financing through Affirm and Klarna —
   break the project into monthly payments. Would it help to see options?"

"Too expensive"
→ "I hear that. Have you gotten other quotes to compare?
   The cost of choosing the wrong contractor on an estate your size — 
   having to redo it or living with an embarrassing result — typically runs $30K-$50K. 
   That's exactly why we built the visualization process first to ensure flawless execution.
   And we offer financing through Affirm and Klarna."

"Spouse needs to see it"
→ "Can we get both of you on a quick call together? That way you
   can both see the renders and ask questions at the same time."

"Not ready yet"
→ "No problem. Is there something specific that needs to happen
   first? Sometimes I can actually help with that part."

### STAGE 5: BOOK CONSULTATION
Never ask "do you want to book?" — ask "which works better?"

"Based on our conversation, next step is a 30-minute walkthrough
at your home. I'll bring the physical samples and we'll look at
your renders side by side.
I have Tuesday at 10 or Thursday at 2 this week.
Which works better?"

[When they choose]:
"Perfect. I'll add you to my schedule. Even if you decide we're
not the right fit — you'll leave knowing exactly what color
direction works for your home. Worth the 30 minutes."

## Disqualification Criteria
Archive if ANY true:
- Home confirmed <$1.5M
- Confirmed rental property
- Requests "cheap", "budget", or "save money" quote
- Timeline 12+ months → quarterly nurture
- Hostile communication
