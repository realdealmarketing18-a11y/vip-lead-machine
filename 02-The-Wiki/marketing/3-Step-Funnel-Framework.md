# 3-Step Funnel Framework — VIP Premier Painting

## Core Principle
Every content asset → 3 ad variations
Each ad targets a different awareness level
Together they create a complete conversion system

## STEP 1 — COLD TRAFFIC (Awareness)
Target: Never heard of VIP Premier
Awareness: Problem aware (know they want to paint, don't know us)

Hook: Problem/curiosity based
"Still guessing what color to paint your home?"
"5 mistakes {{city}} homeowners make choosing exterior paint"
"Why paint swatches are lying to you"

Visual: BEFORE photo (desaturated, flat lighting — pain state)
Length: 15 seconds max
CTA: "Learn more" or "See how"
Objective: Video views or link clicks
Audience: Homeowners in [community] zip, age 35-65, home improvement

## STEP 2 — WARM TRAFFIC (Consideration)
Target: Watched Step 1 or visited website
Awareness: Solution aware (know visualization exists)

Hook: Solution reveal + social proof
"See how {{community}} homeowners transformed their home BEFORE painting"
"How Douglas & Sheri saw 20+ options on their actual home"
"The reason {{community}} homeowners never choose wrong colors"

Visual: Higgsfield render reveal (Before → After transition)
        OR: Episode video excerpt (Step 1 HOOK scene)
Length: 30-60 seconds
CTA: "See examples" or "Watch transformation"
Objective: Engagement or landing page visits
Audience: Step 1 video viewers (50%+) + website visitors

## STEP 3 — HOT TRAFFIC (Conversion)
Target: Engaged with Step 1 + 2
Awareness: Most aware (ready to decide)

Hook: Social proof + urgency + risk reversal
"67 luxury homeowners. 5 communities. Zero color regrets."
"See your home transformed first — then decide. Always free."
"Limited spots available for {{community}} this month"

Visual: Render K (Final CTA Hero — peak golden hour, all upgrades)
        + Before/After gold wipe split
        + Client testimonial quote overlay
Length: 15-30 seconds
CTA: "Get My Free Visualization" → estimate link
Objective: Lead generation
Audience: Step 2 engagers + landing page visitors (no conversion)

## Content Asset → Ad Mapping

| Content Asset | Step 1 | Step 2 | Step 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Higgsfield renders | BEFORE desaturated | Render reveal | Render K hero |
| Episode video | Hook scene (0-30s) | Full episode | CTA scene (4:15-5:00) |
| Client story PDF | Pain point page | Transformation | Results + CTA |
| Community page | Blog post clip | Landing page | Estimate link |
| Swatch cards | "Which color?" hook | Palette reveal | Selected palette |
