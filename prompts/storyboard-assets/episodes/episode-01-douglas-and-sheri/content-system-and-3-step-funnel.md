## 01 · The Non-Negotiable Rule
**Every Video Starts as a Cinematic HGTV Episode — No Exceptions**

A real estate tour opener is vanilla. Forgettable. Boring. It tells people nothing emotional.

Your opener is a story with a real couple — their home, their journey, their transformation — shot and edited to feel like an HGTV episode that stopped them mid-scroll.

The thumbnail shows the couple, their home (BEFORE + AFTER side by side), your face as the host, and the visualizations at the bottom. It looks like a show, not an ad. That's the unfair advantage.

### The VIP Opener Standard — 4 Elements

| Element | Timing | Description |
|---------|--------|-------------|
| 1 · Split Reveal | 0–3 sec | BEFORE left, AFTER right, gold line in center. No intro. No logo. Pure transformation impact. Stops the scroll instantly. |
| 2 · Episode Title Card | 3–8 sec | "EPISODE 1 · DOUGLAS & SHERI" fades in. "Transforming Homes. Transforming Lives." Signals this is a series worth watching. |
| 3 · Meet the Couple | 8–15 sec | Portrait circle with name card. Location badge. "FONTANA, CA" — local viewers see themselves. Emotional investment begins. |
| 4 · Hook Question | 15–30 sec | Narrator's first line creates a curiosity gap so strong they HAVE to keep watching to find out how it was done. |

### What Makes This HGTV-Level

- **Results first** — viewers see the transformation before they know anything about VIP
- **Real couple** — not actors, not generic stock. Douglas & Sheri's actual house, actual portraits
- **Gold split line** — cinematic wipe technique identical to HGTV reveal moments
- **Episode branding** — signals this is part of an ongoing series people should follow
- **Local location badge** — "FONTANA, CA" makes every local homeowner lean in

## 02 · The Conversion System

### Step 1 · Stop the Scroll — The HGTV Episode
**Hook them with the story. Make them feel something.**

**Format + Platform**
- YouTube: 4–5 min long-form episode (hero embed)
- Instagram Reels: 60–90 sec cut-down
- Facebook Feed: 60–90 sec video ad
- TikTok: 30–45 sec highlight reel

**Hook Line** *(spoken + text overlay)*

**Visual Must-Haves**

**Goal:** Watch full video → Follow → Want this for their home

### Step 2 · Build Curiosity — The Engagement Post
**Make them participate. Get them commenting their preference.**

**Format + Platform**

- Comment bait: "Comment STYLE A/B/C/D below"

**Hook Line**

**Visual Must-Haves**
- Their actual house — 4 renders in a 2×2 grid

**Goal:** Comment → Engage → DM conversation starts
