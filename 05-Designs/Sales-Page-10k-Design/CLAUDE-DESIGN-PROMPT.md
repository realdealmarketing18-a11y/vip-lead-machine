### The Aesthetic (The $10k Vibe)
- **3D Pop:** Components should feel tactile. Use subtle drop shadows, smooth scale transforms on hover (`scale: 1.02`), and layered z-index depths so cards "pop" off the background.
- **Minimalistic & Elegant:** Massive use of whitespace. The layout should breathe. Avoid dense text walls. Everything feels precise, curated, and intentionally spaced.
- **Brand Alignment:** 
  - Backgrounds: Elegant Cream (`#F8F7F4`) to crisp White (`#FFFFFF`).
  - Core Typography: Deep Navy (`#1a1f4e`). Serif font for headlines (e.g. `Cormorant Garamond`), clean Sans-Serif for body text (e.g. `Inter`).
  - Accents & CTAs: Signature Orange (`#e8833a`) used sparingly to draw the eye to critical buttons and interactive elements.

### The Rules (Anti-Slop Mode)
1. Do NOT use generic placeholder text. Use the actual VIP sales page copy (e.g., "The Bridges homeowners Douglas & Sheri saved $1,000's by previewing their colors").
2. No gray default buttons or unstyled native inputs. Every element must be fully styled to match the Light-Luxury design system.
3. Every button or interactive card must have a distinct, buttery-smooth transition state.
