## COPY RULES

- Every section has ONE job. Do not let sections bleed into each other.
- The word "visualization" appears at least 6 times on the page.
- Never write "cheap" or "affordable" — write "investment" or "starting from"
- Mention a specific [city] neighborhood at least 3 times — it signals hyper-local expertise
- The hero headline must be testable in a Meta ad — keep it under 10 words
- Paragraph length maximum: 3 sentences. Luxury buyers skim. Every sentence must pull weight.
