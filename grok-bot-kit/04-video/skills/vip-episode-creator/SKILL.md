# Episode Creator

## Episode Format (8 Scenes — LOCKED)
S01 | 0:00-0:30  | HOOK     | Gold split-reveal BEFORE → AFTER
S02 | 0:30-1:00  | MEET     | Introduce homeowners + promise
S03 | 1:00-1:45  | STRUGGLE | Pain — swatches, Pinterest, $1K consultant
S04 | 1:45-2:15  | DISCOVERY| VIP reveals solution, 3 options glimpsed
S05 | 2:15-2:55  | STEP 1   | 6 SW colors named, swatch cards animate
S06 | 2:55-3:45  | STEP 2+UP| 3 palettes + upsell additions
S07 | 3:45-4:15  | STEP 3   | Gold wipe reveal + chosen palette named
S08 | 4:15-5:00  | CTA      | Full-screen hero + book now

## Each Episode Produces
1. Video script (word-for-word, 8 scenes)
2. Render queue (7 renders, 5 credits, proven prompts)
3. Storyboard (frame-by-frame guide)
4. Sales page (WordPress-ready)
5. Email sequence (3-touch using episode)
6. Meta ad brief (15-sec + 60-sec cuts)
