# Job 03 — Ads, reels, hooks, captions, DMs

**Paste `00-SHARED-RULES.md` above this.**

## What this job does
Writes social content that makes someone stop scrolling and then do one thing: call, or send
a DM.

## The difference from page copy
- On pages, the company name is required. **In ads it's banned from the opening line.**
  Open with *her*: her house, her fear.
- Each piece has one goal: a call or a DM. Don't ask for both.

## Steps
1. Get the buyer's own words → skill `vip-research-agent`
   (`research/{city}/07-VOICE.md`).
2. Write hooks, scripts, captions and DMs → skill `vip-copywriter-agent`.
   Use `HEADLINE-FORMULAS.md` Part F in the OC repo.
3. Build the 3-step ad set (name the problem → prove the fix → ask for the 30 minutes) →
   skill `vip-sales-funnel` and `FUNNEL-3-STEP-ADS.md`.
4. Set up the campaign and audiences → skill `vip-ad-campaign-manager`. Draft only.
   **Launching or changing a budget needs Fabian's OK.**

## Kill test, for every headline
"Could a competitor put their logo on this unchanged?" If yes, rewrite it.
No automatic check reviews ads, so you are the check.
