## WHAT CHANGED FROM v1 (and why)

1. **One clean timeline.** v1 jumped from 0:30 to 1:00 and disagreed with the production master. Timings now follow the spoken word count with no gaps.
2. **Colors fixed.** v1 called Newport Fog, Coastal Sand and Estate Navy "Sherwin-Williams options" — they are our palette names. Step 1 now names six real SW colors, and Step 2 names which SW colors make each palette.
3. **Chosen accent is Iron Ore, not Tricorn Black.** The meeting notes and the render queue both build Render C with Iron Ore SW 7069. v1's script said Tricorn Black, so the video would have said one color while showing another. *(If they really switched to Tricorn Black in the session, change it here and re-render C and K.)*
4. **Paint cans, not swatch cards.** Render F and the palette cards are now three paint cans per scheme, per the brand rule.
5. **Every scene split into numbered shots** with a stream tag, so each one becomes one Higgsfield prompt.
6. **Copy tightened to the brand voice**: v1's "!!" is gone, "stunning" and "eliminated all stress" are out (hype / overclaim), and "dramatization" is noted on screen because the footage of the Gallaghers is rendered.
7. **Phone is (949) 418-6461**, the only number allowed in customer-facing copy (OC repo `context/ABOUT-VIP.md`). v1 used (909) 312-5400.
