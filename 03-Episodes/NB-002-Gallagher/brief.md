## Why VIP Premier?

Richard and Elizabeth Gallagher were frozen in design indecision for over 18 months, terrified of making a costly color error on their $8M Pelican Hill estate. Traditional color swatches and standard design agencies failed to give them confidence. By utilizing our **Custom Visualization Service**, we built a photorealistic digital twin of their home, displaying Newport Fog, Coastal Sand, and Estate Navy in realistic golden hour lighting. Seeing their actual estate transformed before paint application gave them 100% confidence to move forward.

---

## Red Flags

* ** HOA Compliance**: The Pelican Hill HOA has strict guidelines regarding Mediterranean paint palettes and exterior finishes. All schemes generated were pre-selected from the HOA-approved Sherwin-Williams collection.
* **Prestige Protection**: The smooth stucco finish and custom wood elements required meticulous preparation. Addressed by our 5-step preparation protocol and premium Sherwin-Williams Emerald paint systems.
