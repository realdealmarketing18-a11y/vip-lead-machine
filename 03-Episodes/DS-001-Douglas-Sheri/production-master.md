## 01 · Commercial Overview

### The Concept
This is an HGTV-style transformation commercial for VIP Premier Painting, built around real homeowners Douglas & Sheri and their estate at 8273 North Fontana. The episode follows a proven story arc: hook the viewer with the prestigious results, introduce the homeowners and their design anxiety, reveal VIP's Custom Color Scheme Visualization process across three simple steps, and close with a stunning transformation and a clear call to action.

The entire piece is engineered for reuse. Every render prompt, every scene structure, and the Canvas template are built once and redeployed across future episodes — Gary's interior, Covenant Hills, Eagle Glen, and beyond. This is the flagship that proves the format.

### The 8-Scene Structure
1. **The Hook — HGTV Cold Open** (0:00–0:30): Results first. Gold split reveal.
2. **Meet Douglas & Sheri + The Promise** (0:30–1:00): Trust. Bypassing redundant fees. 3 steps teased.
3. **The Struggle** (1:00–1:45): Agitate. Swatches, online searches, dead ends.
4. **The Discovery** (1:45–2:15): The pivot. VIP Concierge reveal.
5. **Step 1 — 6 SW Colors** (2:15–2:55): Each premium color named + swatch card.
6. **Step 2 + Upsell** (2:55–3:45): 3 palettes + custom architectural upgrades.
7. **Step 3 — Chosen Palette** (3:45–4:15): Gold wipe before/after reveal.
8. **Result + CTA** (4:15–5:00): Transformation. Book now.
